About The Conference

The Department of Mathematics, Ramakrishna Mission Vivekananda Centenary College, Rahara, Kolkata, cordially invites researchers, academicians, industry practitioners, and students from across the globe to participate in and contribute to the International Conference on Advances in Mathematical Sciences and Applications 2026 (ICAMSA 2026), to be held in fully online mode on 17–18 December 2026.

About The College

Ramakrishna Mission Vivekananda Centenary College (RKMVCC), located in Rahara, Kolkata, is a premier autonomous boys' science college established in 1963 to commemorate Swami Vivekananda's birth centenary. Administered by the Ramakrishna Mission Order and affiliated with West Bengal State University, it offers undergraduate, postgraduate, and doctoral programs across seven core science disciplines. Accredited with a NAAC Grade A++ and consistently ranked among India's top colleges by the National Institutional Ranking Framework (NIRF), RKMVCC is renowned for strict discipline, academic rigor, and Swami Vivekananda's "man-making" educational philosophy.

What is Our Goal?

ICAMSA 2026 aims to provide a premier international virtual forum for sharing recent breakthroughs, theoretical advancements, and real-world applications in mathematical sciences. The conference will feature keynote deliberations by eminent mathematicians, invited talks, and online technical paper presentations designed to foster interdisciplinary collaboration across applied mathematics, computational science, engineering, and data analytics.

Conference Tracks & Scope

Original and unpublished research contributions are invited in, but not limited to, the following areas:

  • Track 1: Applied Mathematics & Mathematical Modeling (Fluid Dynamics, Solid Mechanics, Ecological & Epidemiological Modeling, Nonlinear Dynamics)
  • Track 2: Differential Equations & Dynamical Systems (ODEs, PDEs, Fractional Calculus, Bifurcation & Chaos Theory)
  • Track 3: Numerical Analysis & Scientific Computing (Finite Element Methods, Computational Fluid Dynamics, High-Performance Computing)
  • Track 4: Optimization, Control Theory & Operations Research (Game Theory, Supply Chain Modeling, Stochastic Optimization, Fuzzy Logic)
  • Track 5: Data Science, Machine Learning & Computational Intelligence (Statistical Modeling, Neural Networks, AI in Mathematical Sciences)
  • Track 6: Pure Mathematics & Interdisciplinary Applications (Algebra, Topology, Functional Analysis, Graph Theory, Cryptography, Financial Mathematics)

Publication Opportunities

We are pleased to offer authors multiple avenues to disseminate their research findings and gain wide academic visibility through our official conference publishing partners.

Book of Abstracts (with DOI)

All accepted and presented abstracts—alongside keynote deliberations—will be compiled and published as official conference proceedings in a peer-reviewed journal supplement or special issue. Each published abstract will be assigned a unique Digital Object Identifier (DOI) to ensure permanent archiving, discoverability, and proper academic citation.

Full-Length Journal Publications

Authors of selected high-quality, full-length research papers presented at the conference will be invited to submit extended versions of their work for consideration in reputed peer-reviewed and indexed journals (Scopus / Web of Science), including:

  • Applied Mathematical Biosystems
  • Other leading thematic and indexed journals aligned with the conference tracks

Note on Peer Review: All full-length submissions will be routed according to their thematic category and will undergo the respective journal’s standard, independent peer-review process prior to final acceptance and publication.
